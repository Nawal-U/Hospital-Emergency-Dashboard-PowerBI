# Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard
## Overview
Created a Power BI dashboard to enhance operational efficiency and provide actionable insights into emergency room performance. This solution will enable stakeholders to track, analyze, and make data-driven decisions regarding patient management and service

## Key Metrics Tracked
- Number of Patients
- Average wait time
- Patient Satisfaction score
- Number of Patients Referred

## Actionable Insights & Recommendations for Jan 2024
- Emergency department visits peaked on Mondays, Tuesdays, and Wednesdays, with the highest patient inflow during morning (9-12 PM) and evening (5-8 PM) hours, indicating sustained weekday demand pressure.
- Only 37.5% of patients were seen within 30 minutes, while most patients experienced longer wait times. Introducing appointment-based scheduling for non-critical cases could help reduce congestion and improve service efficiency.
- 53% of patients were not admitted, suggesting a high proportion of non-emergency cases utilizing emergency services. Redirecting these patients to urgent care or general practice could ease emergency department workload.
- Patient visits were most frequent among the 0-9, 30-39, and 70-79 age groups, though the overall age distribution remained relatively balanced, indicating a need for broad service readiness across age groups.
- The majority of referrals were to General Practice, followed by Orthopedics, highlighting an opportunity to optimize specialist scheduling, particularly orthopedic coverage, during peak demand periods.
- Patient volume was evenly distributed by gender, reinforcing that staffing and operational planning should prioritize time-based demand patterns rather than demographic segmentation.
- The patient satisfaction score of 4.90 out of 10 indicates moderate patient experience and could be improved by reducing wait times and aligning doctor availability with peak visit hours.
